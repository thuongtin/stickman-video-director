# Science-lab sample package: “The foam fountain” (3 clips, 16:9, ~30 s)

Four recurring characters: Dr. Nova, Kip, Bolt, Prof. Otto (Juno and Dr. Rocco sit this one out). Hands are named from each character's own side. Props keep one name everywhere: the glass beaker, the round flask, the small yellow cup, the metal tray.

Hook card: Pattern: Reaction before cause. First frame: Kip holds a glass beaker at arm's length, leaning away with his eyes squeezed shut. Hook hit: at about 1.5s a column of pink foam shoots out of the beaker, taller than Kip. Sound cue: a fizzing whoosh. First spoken line: “How did a spoonful of yeast do that?” Open loop: why the foam grows, paid off in Clip 3. Sound-off check: a boy bracing, then a foam fountain.

## Stitching guide

- Clip 1 → clip 2: a hard time cut back to before the foam; the hands lines are marked (time cut). Keep Kip on the center and Dr. Nova on the left in both clips so screen direction holds.
- Clip 2 → clip 3: match on the glass beaker in Kip's hands as the first bubbles rise; cut on the bubbles, not on a hand move.
- Trim each clip at its beat boundaries; check the pour, the cup pick-up and set-down, the beaker hand-off and the jump frame by frame for swapped hands and the yellow band on the right wrist.

## Voice and music continuity

- One narrator: the frozen General American female block, verbatim in all three clips; no character speaks.
- Clip 1 sets the score (light bubbly pizzicato strings around 110 BPM with soft popping bubble sounds); clips 2 and 3 continue it with the same tempo and instrumentation. Crossfade the music across the cuts in post and normalize loudness with the voice on top.
- Terms and numbers (catalase, oxygen, 3%) go in post as overlays from the approved script, never in the clips.
