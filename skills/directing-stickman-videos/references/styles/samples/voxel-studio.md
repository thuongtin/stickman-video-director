# Voxel studio sample package: “Pass it on” (3 clips, 16:9, ~30 s)

Six original chibi voxel characters of one small studio crew; this story uses Khôi, Bit, Mai, Hà and Bác Tùng. Anchors are the `LOOK` texts of src/cast.py, pasted in full. Hands are named from each character's own side; every human wears the orange band on the right wrist, and Bit (no hands) stays out of the hands lines. The score is set once in clip 1 and continues unchanged; each beat names its camera move. The hook follows the Golden Hook section of references/storyboard-template.md (pattern: Physics break). The prompts name no existing brand, game or character. Not yet tested on generated video.

## Global continuity block

- Ratio and length: 16:9, three clips of about ten seconds (about 30 s).
- Style: `voxel-studio` (pack `voxel-studio.md`, status gated). The style paragraph and the body rule after the character anchors are repeated verbatim in every clip.
- Cast: Khôi and Bit (clips 1–3), Mai and Hà (clips 2–3), Bác Tùng (clip 3). Each anchor is repeated verbatim in every clip the character appears in; a clip lists exactly who is in it.
- Locks: orange band on the right wrist; Bit never holds anything; every orange cube stays hand-sized and is held in one named hand, except the cube stack, which Khôi carries in both hands; passes happen on the camera side (giver's right hand to taker's left hand).
- Palette: white studio, black, white and soft greys, navy jeans, warm light skin, orange accents (platform tops, cubes, wrist bands, Mai's hair clip, Bit's ear light).
- Narrator: one off-screen adult male narrator, General American, warm clean baritone; the voice block is identical in all three clips.
- Score: a soft plucked kalimba and light marimba melody over a gentle synth pad at a calm walking tempo, set in clip 1 and continued unchanged in clips 2–3; the hook's sound is a soft pop and a short rolling clatter.
- Continuity: the top piece falls in clip 1; clip 2 opens on the same platforms with the fallen cube where it stopped; clip 3 continues at the gap further along the same path.
- Text: none in the frames (text policy `none`). Captions, if wanted, go in platform captions, never in the picture.

Bodies and limbs keep their size and never stretch or pass through each other. Faces are made of cubes on the front of the head: expressions change only those face cubes, never the head shape. Every human wears one orange band round the right wrist; never on the left. Bit floats a hand's width above the platform, moves only by small hops and never holds anything.

```text
Props: a cube stack of three orange cubes, each about as wide as a hand, one on top of the other; a path of four round orange-topped platforms floating side by side from left to right.

Composition: 16:9 three-quarter view from the front left at chest height of the characters, full-body framing, the platform path across the middle of the frame, Khôi on the second platform walking toward the right.

Hands at start — Khôi: right hand holds the cube stack; left hand holds the cube stack.
First frame: Khôi is already mid-stride on the platform path, carrying the wobbling cube stack in front of his chest, held from below in his right hand and steadied from the side in his left hand; Bit floats on the last platform at the right edge.

Timed beats:
- [0–3s] (VO: "One person, too many tasks, and one falls."): Khôi walks right with the cube stack in his right hand and his left hand. At about 1.5s the top orange cube tips off the cube stack, falls to the platform with a soft pop and rolls to a stop. Khôi stops mid-step, eyes wide behind his glasses. Camera: slow lateral track with Khôi, then hold.
- [3–7s] (VO: "Bit hops over to see what went wrong."): Bit hops in from the right in two small hops and lands beside the fallen cube, its white eyes wide. Khôi turns his head toward Bit. Camera: hold.
- [7–10s] (VO: "Maybe the problem is doing it all alone."): Khôi sets the cube stack down on the platform and lets go with both hands, then shrugs: his forearms lift out to the sides with the elbows bent, palms up. Bit tilts its round body. Camera: slow push-in on Khôi and Bit.

Dialogue: audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes. Speak exactly the quoted lines once, in order; do not add, omit, paraphrase, repeat, caption, subtitle or display any words.

Voice: One off-screen adult male narrator throughout. General American accent, warm clean baritone, medium-low pitch, gentle chest resonance, no raspiness or breathiness. Calm conversational delivery, restrained pitch variation, steady speaking pace, and close, dry studio sound. Keep the same vocal timbre, register, accent, pace, and recording quality throughout. Emotional progression belongs to the visuals and music; do not change the narrator's voice or switch to character dialogue.

Audio: a soft plucked kalimba and light marimba melody over a gentle synth pad at a calm walking tempo; soft woody steps on the platforms; at 1.5s one soft pop and a short rolling clatter, then a beat of near-silence; two light hop sounds for Bit.

Final frame: Khôi stands beside the cube stack, now two orange cubes, with empty hands, shrugging; Bit floats beside the fallen cube.
Hands at end — Khôi: right hand empty; left hand empty.

Negative constraints: no text, no letters or numbers anywhere, no signs with words, no screens with writing, no game interface, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark, no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes, no existing cartoon or video-game characters, no pixel textures on the cube faces, no shiny plastic, no round studs, no outdoor scenery, no strong colours other than orange, no fingers, no stretched limbs, no limbs passing through bodies, no wristband on the left wrist, no hand or held cube covering a face, no cubes growing or shrinking, no morphing, no duplicate characters, no realistic human bodies, no photoreal people, no 2D cartoon outlines, no knees or elbows bending backward, no limbs changing length, no head turned past the shoulders, no swapped left and right hands, no prop switching hands without a visible pass.
```

## Stitching guide

1. Clip 1 → Clip 2: clip 1 ends with Khôi shrugging beside the two-cube stack and Bit beside the fallen cube; clip 2 opens on the same platforms as Mai and Hà step in from the right. Hard cut on Mai's wave; 4–6 frame audio crossfade on the kalimba.
2. Clip 2 → Clip 3: hard cut along the same platform path to the gap. What continues: Khôi, Mai and Hà holding their cubes in the same hands, Bit, the white studio. What changes: Bác Tùng waits on the far platform.
3. If a cube grows or shrinks as it settles into the gap, trim clip 3 to cut on the click.
4. Inspect each real join: if a clip ends on a different hand or side of the frame, trim to the last frame that matches the Hands at end line rather than trusting the prompt. Check every frame for text, logos, a glossy plastic look or pixel textures on the cubes before publishing.

## Voice and music continuity note

- Every clip repeats the same narrator paragraph word for word, and clips 2–3 say the kalimba and marimba continue from clip 1 with identical tempo and instrumentation. Check by listening to the three clips back to back.
- If the voice or score drifts, first retest a voice-only change with the visuals, dialogue and music held fixed; the fallback is one continuous external English VO and music track laid over the assembled picture.
- Post-production: keep the white backdrop clean (no colour grade that tints it); captions only as platform captions.
