# Đông Hồ sample package: “The cake that came back” (3 clips, 16:9, ~30 s)

Four recurring characters: Mận, Tí, Ông Đồ, Cô Thắm. Hands are named from each character's own side.

## Global continuity block

- Ratio and length: 16:9, three clips of about ten seconds (about 30 s).
- Style: `dong-ho` (Đông Hồ folk woodblock print, draft pack `dong-ho.md`, status gated, not tried on Flow). The style paragraph and the line after the character anchors are repeated verbatim in every clip.
- Cast: Mận (clips 1–3), Tí (clips 1 and 3), Ông Đồ (clips 2–3), Cô Thắm (clip 3). Each anchor is the frozen text from `src/rig.py`, repeated verbatim in every clip the character appears in; a clip lists exactly who is in it.
- Palette: paper white plus cinnabar red, pagoda-flower yellow, leaf green, indigo blue, soft brick pink and charcoal black.
- Narrator: one off-screen adult female narrator, gentle Northern Vietnamese accent speaking English, chosen for this video (the style does not fix a narrator). The voice block is identical in all three clips.
- Score: gentle bamboo flute melody over a soft wooden clapper keeping a slow beat, set in clip 1 and continued unchanged in clips 2–3; the hook's sound is one soft gong note.
- Continuity: the rice cake travels Mận → Ông Đồ across clips 1–2; clip 3 opens on a named time cut to the afternoon at the same porch; the red paper painting travels Ông Đồ → Mận in clip 3.
- Text: none in the frames. Captions, a title or Hán Nôm calligraphy are added in post-production (text policy `post-overlay`).

## Stitching guide

1. Clip 1 → Clip 2: clip 1 ends with Mận at the centre facing right holding the rice cake in both hands; clip 2 opens with Mận walking in from the left holding it the same way (same direction of travel, same grip). Use a hard cut on her step; a 4–6 frame audio crossfade on the flute.
2. Clip 2 → Clip 3: approved time cut to the afternoon at the same porch. What continues: Ông Đồ, Mận, the low red table and the rice cake (now resting on the table). What changes: time of day, the brush in Ông Đồ's right hand, Cô Thắm and Tí join. Hard cut; no crossfade on the picture.
3. Inspect each real join: if a generated clip ends on a different grip or a different side of the frame, trim to the last frame where the hands match the Hands at end line rather than trusting the prompt.

## Voice and music continuity note

- Every clip repeats the same narrator paragraph word for word, and clips 2–3 say the flute and clapper continue from clip 1 with the same tempo and instrumentation. Check by listening to the three clips back to back, not from stills.
- If the voice drifts, first retest a voice-only change with the visuals, dialogue and music held fixed; one continuous external English VO and flute/clapper track laid over the assembled picture is the fallback.
- Post-production: add any caption, title or calligraphy as an overlay on the paper areas (top band or side margins), never in the prompts; keep the double border visible; do not colour-grade (it breaks the flat pigments).
