# Xóm Ao — "Save my seat" (vn-webcomic, 3 clips, 16:9, ~30 s)

Sample package for the vn-webcomic style draft. Hook pattern: Reaction before cause (references/opening-hook.md). Post-production overlay (optional, not in prompts): none needed, the joke reads muted.

## Clip 1 — Hook: the guarded stool

```text
Output: approximately ten seconds, 16:9, 720p target, 24 FPS, synchronized audio.

Opening hook: Cóc stands side-on in a quiet alley holding a red plastic stool out in front of his chest with both arms straight, the stool lifted off the ground, arms trembling, his head turned to stare at the viewer with a flat deadpan face; at about 1.5s Cô Vịt walks into frame with a glass of iced tea, sees him, and her dot eyes snap into two big empty white circles.

Style and environment: a flat 2D cartoon in the look of a Vietnamese social-media comic strip brought to motion, original animal characters only. Characters are small round animals about two heads tall: big round heads, soft bean-shaped bodies, short noodle arms and legs of even width, round mitten hands, and one thick uniform black outline around every shape. Flat pastel color fills only: no shading, no gradients, no texture, no lighting effects. Faces are tiny and calm: two small black dot eyes and a small mouth. For a big reaction the dot eyes snap into two large empty white circles, or white circles with a pinpoint pupil, and later snap back; small comic marks drawn on the face (a sweat drop, pink blush) are allowed. Backgrounds are a simple Vietnamese alley in flat pastel shapes: a warm cream wall, a shopfront with a rolled-up shutter and a hanging bulb, a small low green tea table, red plastic stools. Reaction shots use a plain flat pastel color with thin speed lines from the edges. Comic-strip timing: a character holds a pose, then snaps quickly into the next; each punchline lands after a held, silent beat of about one second.

Characters (exactly 2, no one else): Cóc, a small round green toad: a wide flat head almost as wide as his body, two tiny black dot eyes set far apart, a very wide thin mouth, a pale yellow-green belly, a loose white sleeveless undershirt, bare green legs with wide flat feet, and a red rubber band on the right wrist. Cô Vịt, a white duck who runs the iced-tea stall: a round white head with a wide flat orange bill, tiny black dot eyes, a small curl of feathers on top, a purple long-sleeved áo bà ba blouse with two front pockets, loose black trousers, orange webbed feet, and a red rubber band on the right wrist. Every character has exactly two arms and two legs, each arm ending in one round hand; a moving limb replaces its previous pose. Limbs keep their length and bend smoothly at elbows and knees. Every character wears a red rubber band on the right wrist only. Characters stand at least an arm's length apart; heads, bills and snouts never touch or overlap.

Props: one red plastic stool: a low round seat on a flared base with a round cut-out in the side, no logo; one ribbed clear glass of iced tea with amber tea and ice cubes.

Composition: 16:9 wide shot of the alley, side-on, Cóc standing at the left beside the small low tea table; Cô Vịt enters from the right edge. Then a hard cut to a chest-up shot of Cóc on a flat pale pink background with thin speed lines. Full bodies in the wide shot.

Hands at start — Cóc: right hand holds the red plastic stool; left hand holds the red plastic stool. Cô Vịt: right hand empty; left hand holds the glass of iced tea.
First frame: Cóc already stands side-on at the left facing right, head turned to the viewer, holding the red plastic stool out in front of his chest with both arms straight, the stool lifted off the ground, arms shaking a little, a sweat drop on his face, tiny dot eyes, mouth a flat line.

Timed beats:
- [0–3s] (VO: "My friend asked me to save his seat."): Camera holds on the wide shot. Cóc holds the stool out in front of him, perfectly still except for trembling arms. At about 1.5s Cô Vịt walks in from the right, facing left, the glass of iced tea in her left hand; she stops a full arm's length from Cóc and her eyes snap into big empty white circles.
- [3–7s] (VO: "So I saved it. Nobody has taken it."): Hard cut to Cóc, chest-up on the flat pink background, still holding the stool out in front of his chest in both hands; he gives one small proud nod. Cut back to the wide shot: Cô Vịt sets down the glass of iced tea on the low tea table with her left hand and steps back.
- [7–10s] (VO: "Ten minutes now."): Camera holds on the wide shot. Silence for one second while Cóc stays frozen holding the stool; then his arms give one big wobble, he steadies them, and keeps staring at the viewer with dot eyes.

Dialogue: audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes. Speak exactly the quoted lines once, in order; do not add, omit, paraphrase, repeat, caption, subtitle or display any words.

Voice: One off-screen young adult male narrator throughout, telling his own story in the first person. Light Northern Vietnamese accent speaking clear English, soft light tenor, medium pitch, slightly nasal forward resonance, clean texture with no raspiness or breathiness. Sincere, completely deadpan delivery, as if every sentence were perfectly reasonable; flat pitch with small pauses before the last words of a joke. Steady unhurried pace, close and dry studio sound. Keep the same vocal timbre, register, accent, pace and recording quality throughout. Emotional progression belongs to the visuals and music; do not change the narrator's voice or switch to character dialogue. No character's mouth moves with the words.

Audio: voiceover clearly audible over a light plucky đàn bầu-and-ukulele loop at a slow walking tempo; soft footsteps for Cô Vịt, a small glass clink on the table; the music drops out for the one-second silent beat, then a tiny wobble boing.

Final frame: Cóc at the left, stool still held out in front of his chest in both hands, deadpan; Cô Vịt at the right with empty hands, eyes back to dots; the glass of iced tea stands on the tea table.
Hands at end — Cóc: right hand holds the red plastic stool; left hand holds the red plastic stool. Cô Vịt: right hand empty; left hand empty.

Negative constraints: no 3D render, no CGI, no anime or manga style, no realistic animals or realistic anatomy, no shading or gradients, no paper texture, no resemblance to any existing comic or webcomic character, no hats, no extra arms, legs or fingers, no duplicate characters, no extra animals in the background, no knees or elbows bending backward, no limbs changing length, no head turned past the shoulders, no swapped left and right hands, no prop switching hands without a visible pass, no heads, bills or snouts overlapping, no visible text, letters, numbers or signboards, no logos, no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes, no emoji or symbols floating in the air, no watermark.
```
