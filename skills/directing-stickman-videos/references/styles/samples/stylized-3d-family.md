# The red ball — stylized 3D family prompt package

Stylized 3D family-film look, 3 clips, 16:9, about 30 seconds. Cast: Lulu, Ray, Su, Linh (at most three characters per clip; the Vietnamese names Bé Su and Mẹ Linh stay out of the prompts so each character has one spelling).
Hands are named from each character's own side. Every character wears one yellow band on the right wrist only.

### Clip 1 — The ball comes back

```text
Create an approximately 10-second 16:9 horizontal stylized 3D animated family-film clip targeting 720p at 24 FPS with synchronized audio.

Style: a stylized 3D animated feature-film look for families. Appealing exaggerated, shape-based proportions: oversized heads on kids, one boldly pushed shape on adults (a round belly, a long neck, a tall thin frame), soft rounded simplified forms, no realistic anatomy. Skin is smooth and satin with a soft warm glow where light meets shadow, no pores, wrinkles, veins or visible nostrils. Big eyes with one solid iris color, a black pupil and one bright catchlight; soft sculpted eyebrows; simple mouths. Hair is a few large sculpted clumps of one color, never individual strands. Clothes are plain matte cotton with no prints, text or logos. Warm soft key light from the upper left, a cool soft fill, a gentle warm rim light, soft contact shadows. Smooth, weighty, readable acting with clear poses and slight squash and stretch. Palette: warm cream walls and honey-wood floors, soft sunlit gold, clear but gentle costume colors (coral, teal, purple, rust orange, sky blue, moss green), deep warm browns for hair, no neon colors and no grey, desaturated grading.

Setting: a cozy family living room in late afternoon: warm wooden floor, a cream wall with one bright window, a low wooden crate by the wall. Lulu walks in from the left; Ray stands right of center facing left; Su stands at the back on the right, beside Ray and away from the pass.

Characters, repeated exactly in every clip: Lulu: an eight-year-old girl, about three heads tall. Large round head, warm light-tan skin, rosy cheeks, big dark-brown eyes; dark-brown hair in two short puffy pigtails low at the sides of her head, tied with coral hair ties, and a short fringe. Coral short-sleeved t-shirt under teal overall shorts; white sneakers with red soles. Ray: an eleven-year-old boy, about four heads tall, lanky. Warm brown skin, big dark-brown eyes, a rounded cap of tight dark curls. Purple sweatshirt with slightly darker plum sleeves, charcoal trousers, orange sneakers. Su: a three-year-old boy, about two and a half heads tall, very round head, light-tan skin, rosy cheeks, huge dark-brown eyes, short dark-brown hair with one curl standing up on top. Green long-sleeved t-shirt, navy shorts, red shoes. Every character has exactly two arms and two legs and cartoon hands with three fingers and a thumb. Elbows and knees bend one way only and limbs never stretch. Every character wears one bright yellow band on the right wrist only.

Props: one red rubber ball.

First frame: the living room with Ray standing right of center facing left and Su at the back on the right; the red rubber ball is already bouncing in from the left edge of the frame.
Hands at start — Lulu: right hand empty; left hand empty. Ray: right hand empty; left hand empty. Su: right hand empty; left hand empty.

[0–3s] Camera: slow lateral track following Lulu from left to right. The red rubber ball bounces once on the floor; Lulu walks in behind it and picks up the red rubber ball with her right hand at about 1s. Lulu carries the red rubber ball in her right hand toward Ray.
[3–7s] Camera: hold a medium-wide shot during the pass. Lulu stops in front of Ray and holds out the red rubber ball in her right hand. Ray reaches with his left hand, and Lulu passes the red rubber ball from her right hand to Ray's left hand at about 6s; both hands touch the ball at the pass.
[7–10s] Camera: slow push-in toward Ray. Ray lifts the red rubber ball high in his left hand and grins toward the camera; Lulu laughs with both hands down; Su jumps in place with both arms up, clear of the ball.
Hands at end — Lulu: right hand empty; left hand empty. Ray: right hand empty; left hand holds the red rubber ball. Su: right hand empty; left hand empty.

Final frame: Ray center-right holding the red rubber ball up in his left hand, Lulu on the left laughing, Su behind Ray on the right with both arms up.

Dialogue: audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes. Speak exactly the quoted lines once, in order; do not add, omit, paraphrase, repeat, caption, subtitle or display any words: “Every summer the same red ball rolled out of our house, and every summer Ray brought it back.”

One off-screen adult female narrator throughout. General American accent, warm clear mid-range voice, gentle forward resonance, no raspiness or breathiness. Calm, smiling conversational delivery, restrained pitch variation, steady speaking pace, and close, dry studio sound. Keep the same vocal timbre, register, accent, pace, and recording quality throughout. Emotional progression belongs to the visuals and music; do not change the narrator's voice or switch to character dialogue.

Music/SFX: light ukulele and soft glockenspiel around 100 BPM; a loud rubbery boing on the first bounce, sneaker squeaks on the floor; keep the voice dominant.

Do not generate photorealistic people, realistic skin pores, wrinkles or stubble, glossy plastic or doll-like skin, small realistic eyes, individual hair strands, uncanny-valley faces, likenesses of existing animated characters, extra fingers, extra arms or legs, duplicate characters, unintended characters, a raised hand, arm or ball covering anyone's face, changed proportions or colors between shots, the yellow wrist band on the left wrist, visible writing, letters, numbers, captions, subtitles, speech bubbles, dialogue boxes, logos or watermarks. No knees or elbows bending backward, no limbs changing length, no head turned past the shoulders, no swapped left and right hands, no prop switching hands without a visible pass. Do not alter, omit, repeat, reorder, or add dialogue.
```
