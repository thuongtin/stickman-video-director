# Blueprint sample package: “The missing gear” (3 clips, 16:9, ~30 s)

Four recurring characters: Nova, Mira, Hank, Cog. Hands are named from each character's own side.
Post-production text (optional): none is needed; any title goes in the blank title block in the edit.

## Global continuity block

- Ratio and length: 16:9, three clips of about ten seconds (about 30 s).
- Style: `blueprint` (Engineering Blueprint, style pack `blueprint.md`, status gated). The style paragraph, the character anchors and the common rules after them are repeated verbatim in every clip.
- Cast: Nova (clips 1–3), Mira (clips 1 and 3), Hank (clips 2–3), Cog (clip 3). Each anchor is the frozen text from `src/rig.py`, repeated verbatim in every clip the character appears in; a clip lists exactly who is in it.
- Locks: every character wears one solid white cuff band on the right wrist only; Cog keeps its two round lens eyes and slot mouth; one name per prop for the whole package: the wrench, the clipboard, the big gear, the small box (never "the cog": Cog is a character).
- Palette: Prussian blue and the white of the paper only; no accent colours.
- Narrator: one off-screen adult female narrator, neutral American accent, chosen for this video (the style does not fix a narrator). The voice block is identical in all three clips.
- Score: a light ticking clockwork rhythm with soft pizzicato strings, set in clip 1 and continued unchanged in clips 2–3; sound cues are a plotter scratch, metal clicks and a whir, never a bass hit.
- Continuity: the wrench stays in Nova's right hand through clips 1–2 until she tucks it into her bib pocket; the big gear travels Hank → Nova in clip 2 and onto the empty shaft in clip 3; the machine, grid, border and title block stay in the same place.
- Text: none in the frames. Dimension capsules and the title block stay blank and are filled in post-production (text policy `blank-zone`).

## Stitching guide

1. Clip 1 → Clip 2: Nova's grip at the end of clip 1 is the same as at the start of clip 2 (compare the Hands lines); clip 2 opens with Nova at the right while Hank walks in from the left. Hard cut on Hank's first step; a 4–6 frame audio crossfade on the clockwork rhythm.
2. Clip 2 → Clip 3: Nova's two-hand grip at the end of clip 2 carries into clip 3, which opens with her below the empty shaft, Mira and Hank on the front line and Cog on the back row. Hard cut; no crossfade on the picture.
3. Inspect each real join: if a generated clip ends on a different grip, a cuff on the wrong wrist or a prop in a different place, trim to the last frame where the hands match the Hands at end line rather than trusting the prompt. Check that the grid and title block do not jump at the cut.

## Voice and music continuity note

- Every clip repeats the same narrator paragraph word for word, and clips 2–3 say the clockwork rhythm and pizzicato strings continue from clip 1 with the same tempo and instrumentation. Check by listening to the three clips back to back, not from stills.
- If the voice drifts, first retest a voice-only change with the visuals, dialogue and music held fixed; one continuous external English VO and clockwork track laid over the assembled picture is the fallback.
- Post-production: fill the blank dimension capsules and the title block as the style pack says, add subtitles below the lower ground line, keep the double border visible, and do not colour-grade (any tint breaks the one-pigment look).
