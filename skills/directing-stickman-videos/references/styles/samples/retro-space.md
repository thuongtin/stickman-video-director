# Why astronauts float — retro space-age spec package

Retro space-age poster style, multi-character. 16:9, 3 clips, about 30 seconds. Voice: the accepted male narrator block. Every astronaut wears the crew's left/right marker, a round mustard gauge on the RIGHT wrist, repeated in every anchor. Science check: astronauts float because they are in free fall around the Earth, not because there is no gravity up there (gravity at station height is still about 90% of the ground value).

Hook card: pattern Physics break. First frame: Juno floats in a station cabin holding a red apple. Hook hit at about 1s: she lets go and the apple simply stays in mid-air. Sound cue: the music drops to a single vibraphone note on the release. First spoken line: "This apple is falling right now." Open loop: why does it not fall? Paid off in clip 3. Sound-off check: an apple hanging in mid-air beside a child astronaut reads with no audio.

A loose object stays exactly where it is let go and keeps drifting at the speed it was released. No swimming strokes, nothing falls to the floor, hair stays inside the helmets.

Vega, the tall mission commander: a round clear bubble helmet, inside it a warm brown face with dot eyes, small floating eyebrows and a short black bob; a padded burnt-orange spacesuit with broad rounded shoulders, a narrow waist with a cream belt, a cream chest box with three coloured lamps, ribbed cream rings at the elbows and knees, a cream backpack, cream mitten gloves with a thumb, cream boots, and a round mustard-yellow gauge on her right wrist. Rusty, the broad flight engineer: a round clear bubble helmet, inside it a cheerful face with short copper hair and a short copper beard; a bulky mustard-yellow spacesuit with orange rings at the elbows and knees, an orange belt, an orange chest box with three coloured lamps, a navy backpack, navy mitten gloves with a thumb, navy boots, and a round mustard-yellow gauge on his right wrist. Every character keeps exactly two arms and two legs of constant length; a moving limb replaces its previous pose. Each astronaut's round mustard-yellow gauge stays on that astronaut's right wrist in every shot. Expressions change only the eyebrows, eyes and mouth inside the helmet, never the helmet, suit colours or proportions.

Props: one shiny red apple with a short brown stem and one small teal leaf.

Composition: 16:9, side-on like a poster: Juno floats left of the porthole facing right, Rusty at the right wall facing left, holding a handrail at shoulder height with his left hand, that arm straight out in front of him below his helmet; Vega enters later from the right edge facing left; all full-body. - [0–3s] (VO: "This apple is falling right now."): At about 1s Juno lets go of the red apple from her right hand and pulls the hand back to her chest; the red apple stays exactly where it was, hanging in mid-air beside her and drifting very slowly. Rusty, still holding the handrail with his left hand, stares at the apple with wide eyes.
- [3–7s] (VO: "So is Juno. So is everyone on board."): Vega drifts in from the right edge in a straight line at a steady speed, body in the relaxed floating crouch, and reaches toward the apple with her left hand.
- [7–10s] (VO: "They all fall together, so nothing seems to fall."): Vega catches the red apple with her left hand and grabs the low handrail with her right hand. She stops a hand's width from her crewmate, the two helmets never touching. Juno laughs; Rusty laughs, his left hand still on the handrail.

Dialogue: audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes. Speak exactly the quoted lines once, in order; do not add, omit, paraphrase, repeat, caption, subtitle or display any words.

Voice: One off-screen adult male narrator throughout. General American accent, warm clean baritone, medium-low pitch, gentle chest resonance, no raspiness or breathiness. Calm conversational delivery, restrained pitch variation, steady speaking pace, and close, dry studio sound. Keep the same vocal timbre, register, accent, pace, and recording quality throughout. Emotional progression belongs to the visuals and music; do not change the narrator's voice or switch to character dialogue.

Final frame: Vega floats still with the red apple in her left hand and her right hand on the low handrail. Juno floats a hand's width away and laughs; Rusty holds his handrail at shoulder height.

1. Clip 1 → clip 2, location cut (cabin → tiny planet): Juno's coral suit and the music carry over; Vega and Rusty stay in the cabin. Hands lines restart after the cut (marked time cut).
