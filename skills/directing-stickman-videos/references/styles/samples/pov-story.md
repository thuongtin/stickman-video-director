# POV story sample package: "Part 1 — the vanishing parcels" (3 clips, 9:16, ~30 s)

Six original characters live in one apartment building; this part uses Jess (the "you" of the caption), Marcus (the building manager) and Derek (the neighbour across the hall). Hands are named from each character's own side. The hook follows the Golden Hook section of references/storyboard-template.md (pattern: Reaction before cause; payoff in Clip 3, which also opens Part 2). No account, creator, platform or brand is named. Built as a code-rendered motion test (`motion/motion.mp4` in the style's research folder), not yet as a full 30-second video.

## Global continuity block

- Ratio and length: 9:16, three clips of about ten seconds (about 30 s), 24 FPS, 1080p.
- Style: `pov-story` (flat 2D vertical drama series, pack `pov-story.md`, status gated). The style paragraph, the anchors, the body rule, each speaker's voice line and the negatives are repeated verbatim in every clip.
- Cast: Jess (clips 1–3), Marcus (clip 2), Derek (clip 3). A clip lists exactly who is in it.
- Locks: plain white heads with no nose; Jess's dark-brown blunt fringe and lavender sweatshirt; the photo goes Marcus (right hand) → Jess (left hand) in clip 2 and stays in Jess's left hand through clip 3; Derek holds the parcel in both hands.
- Palette: warm peach hallway, powder-blue mailroom, Jess's lavender, Marcus's navy and khaki, Derek's maroon and blond; nothing shaded.
- Voices: approved character dialogue. Jess, Marcus and Derek each have one frozen voice line, repeated verbatim in every clip where they speak; nobody narrates.
- Score: light pizzicato strings and a soft synth pad at about 96 BPM, set in clip 1 and continued unchanged in clips 2–3; buzzes, the latch and the sting are sound effects.
- Continuity: clip 1 → clip 2 is a named time cut (Jess has walked down to the mailroom; the phone is put away off screen, so clip 2's start line is marked `(time cut)`); clip 2 → clip 3 is a time cut back to her door with the photo still in her left hand.
- Text: none in the art. Overlay list, added by code in post (text policy `post-overlay`): the caption box from 0 s to the end, two lines and a part line: "POV: your packages keep" / "vanishing from your door..." / "Part 1"; word-by-word subtitles of each quoted line on its timing; a "Part 2" tag over the last second of clip 3.

## Stitching guide

1. Clip 1 → Clip 2: time cut. What continues: Jess's look and her annoyed mood; the score. What changes: the set (hallway → mailroom), Jess's phone is gone (put away off screen), Marcus is behind the counter. Hard cut on Jess's held deadpan stare; a 4–6 frame audio crossfade on the pizzicato bed.
2. Clip 2 → Clip 3: time cut. What continues: the photo in Jess's left hand, her wide eyes. What changes: back to the hallway, the door across the hall is shut. Hard cut on the punch-in face.
3. Trim each clip at a held face, never mid-pass. Conform all clips to 24 FPS before joining.
4. Inspect each real join: if a rendered clip has the photo in the wrong hand, a nose or skin colour on a head, or any drawn text, trim to the last frame where the hands match the "Hands at end" line and fix the layer.
5. Lay the overlay layer last: caption box, subtitles on the dialogue timing, the "Part 2" tag.

## Voice and music continuity note

- Each speaker's voice line is identical in every clip they speak in (Jess in clips 1–2, Marcus in clip 2, Derek in clip 3), and clips 2–3 say the pizzicato and synth-pad bed continues from clip 1 with the same tempo and instrumentation. Check by listening to the three clips back to back.
- Record each character's lines with their own frozen profile, cut them to the beats, and lay the score as one continuous bed under the assembled picture, about 14 dB under the dialogue.
- Post-production: buzz, paper slide, whoosh, latch and sting on their beats; subtitles follow the recorded word timing, not the spec's beat boundaries.
