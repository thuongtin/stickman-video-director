# Paper cutout cast package: fixed (after hand_ledger and package_lint)

Three standalone prompts, 16:9, three characters from two paper families (Mai 5A; Ông Bảo and Bé Na 5B), joined by the mixed-cast sentence. Cards, locks, score and negatives are copied from ../../paper-cutout-collage.md by make_cast_package.py. Hands are named from each character's own side.

| Hook card | |
|---|---|
| Pattern | Partial reveal |
| First frame (0.0s) | Close view of the yellow paper balloon above Mai's right hand as she walks right |
| Hook hit | About 1.5s: the foreground grass strip slides away and the pull-back reveals Bé Na staring up at the balloon |
| Sound cue | One soft music-box note on the reveal |
| First spoken line | Some gifts fit in one hand. (six words) |
| Open loop and payoff | Who gets the balloon? Clip 2 hands it over; clip 3 shows what Mai keeps |
| Sound-off check | A girl walks with a balloon and a small child stares at it |

Post-production overlay: none. Stitch clips in order; the final frame of each clip is the first frame of the next.

Voice and music continuity: the same adult female narrator block is repeated word for word in all three clips (voice-continuity.md). Clip 1 sets the 5B score (music box, fingerpicked guitar, glockenspiel, about 84 BPM); clips 2 and 3 say it continues from clip 1 with identical tempo and instrumentation. Listen across the joins after generation; an external VO and music track is the fallback if the narrator drifts.

Screen direction: Mai and Ông Bảo face right, Bé Na faces left. Facing left, Bé Na's right hand is her far hand, so the balloon is taken and held by the arm behind her body.

### Clip 1 — The park path

```text
Create an approximately 10-second 16:9 horizontal paper cutout stop-motion clip targeting 720p at 24 FPS with synchronized audio.

Flat two-dimensional paper cutout stop-motion animation shot straight down onto a tabletop. Every character, prop and scenery element is a separate rigid piece of matte paper with visible paper fibre and slightly irregular cut or torn edges, layered with small soft contact shadows between layers. One warm key light from the upper left. No drawn outlines.

Construction-paper palette: mustard, brick red, navy, teal, cream and white paper with black paper details, flat colours with no gradients. Collage palette: kraft brown, newsprint grey, dusty pink, warm ochre, sky blue and cream papers; patterns such as knit, polka dots, plaid and pinstripes appear on clothing and scenery, never on faces.

This video mixes two kinds of paper puppet in one frame: construction-paper characters show small brass split pins at their joints, while collage characters hide their joints under overlapping paper pieces. Both kinds share the same matte paper fibre, cut edges, contact shadows and warm light.

Animated on twos like hand-moved stop motion: about twelve distinct held poses per second with slight hand-placed jitter between poses. Pieces only rotate around their joints, slide across the table, or are swapped for replacement pieces; limbs stay rigid and never bend or stretch. Depth comes from paper layers sliding at different speeds. The camera stays parallel to the table and never orbits.

Mai, a young woman in her twenties, one flat paper cutout puppet made of separate rigid pieces of matte construction paper, tall and slim, about three heads tall: a tall oval light-tan paper head with long straight black paper hair falling past her shoulders and a small yellow paper hair clip above her temple, two white oval paper eyes with black paper-dot pupils, short black paper eyebrow strips, two coral paper cheek circles and a small black paper mouth piece; a sleeveless teal paper dress with a darker waist seam that flares into an A-line skirt; bare light-tan paper arms, each an upper-arm piece and a forearm piece with a round mitten hand; light-tan paper legs and white paper shoes; small brass split pins at the shoulders, elbows and knees.

Ông Bảo, a grandfather in his seventies, one flat collage paper puppet made of separate rigid pieces of mixed paper, short and slightly stooped, about two and a half heads tall: a pear-shaped light-tan paper head, wider at the jaw and bald on top, with two fluffy white paper tufts of hair above the ears, two thin brown forehead lines, round black paper wire glasses, white paper eyebrows, a white paper moustache and rose paper cheek circles; a brown knitted-pattern paper cardigan with three pale buttons and one patch pocket, open over a cream shirt; sleeves cut from darker brown knitted paper so the arms stand out against the cardigan, ending in light-tan mitten hands; grey pinstriped paper trousers and dark brown paper shoes; joints hidden under overlapping paper pieces.

Bé Na, a three-year-old girl, one flat collage paper puppet made of separate rigid pieces of mixed paper, very small, about two heads tall: a big round pale peach paper head with short dark brown paper hair, a fringe and two round pigtail buns tied with small red paper ties, two white oval paper eyes with black paper-dot pupils, pink paper cheek circles and a small mouth piece; a short sleeveless pink paper dress printed with white polka dots; short bare pale peach paper arms with round mitten hands, the shoulders showing below the head; bare pale peach legs and red paper shoes; joints hidden under overlapping paper pieces.

Set layers: a sky blue card sky with one cream paper cloud, two torn green paper hills, a kraft brown paper park path with a small brown paper bench on the left, and a torn dark green grass strip in the foreground.

Props: one yellow paper balloon on a thin grey string, one red spiral sketchbook with a yellow paper pencil tucked in its spiral, one brown wooden paper cane. No other props.

First frame: close view of Mai mid-step in side view facing right, the yellow paper balloon bobbing above her right hand, a torn grass strip passing in the foreground.
Hands at start — Mai: right hand holds the yellow paper balloon; left hand holds the red sketchbook. Ông Bảo: right hand holds the brown paper cane; left hand empty. Bé Na: right hand empty; left hand empty.

[0–3s] Mai walks right along the path in side view, the yellow paper balloon above her right hand and the red sketchbook in her left hand. At about 1.5s the foreground grass strip slides off to the left and the camera pulls back to the wide table shot, revealing Bé Na on the right staring up at the balloon, her eyes swapped to the wide pieces.
[3–7s] Lateral track to the left: Ông Bảo stands by the bench, leaning on the brown paper cane in his right hand, and waves at Mai with his empty left hand, elbow low and forearm up.
[7–10s] The camera holds: Bé Na points at the balloon with her empty left hand; Mai notices, stops in front of her and smiles as her mouth piece swaps to the open smile.

Audio-only dialogue, exactly once: "Some gifts fit in one hand. Mai's was tied to a string, and someone small had noticed." Audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes. Speak the quoted line exactly once; do not add, omit, paraphrase, repeat, reorder, caption, subtitle or visually transcribe any words.

One off-screen adult female narrator throughout. General American accent, clear warm mid-high voice, gentle forward resonance, no raspiness or breathiness. Lively conversational delivery, controlled pitch variation, steady speaking pace, and close, dry studio sound. Keep the same vocal timbre, register, accent, pace, and recording quality throughout. Emotional progression belongs to the visuals and music; do not change the narrator's voice or switch to character dialogue.

Music: a gentle music-box melody over soft fingerpicked acoustic guitar and light glockenspiel at about 84 BPM, warm and unhurried. SFX: soft paper rustle on moves, light taps when pieces land. At about 1.5s a single soft music-box note lands with the reveal.

Final frame: wide table shot; Mai stands facing right in front of Bé Na, Ông Bảo by the bench on the left.
Hands at end — Mai: right hand holds the yellow paper balloon; left hand holds the red sketchbook. Ông Bảo: right hand holds the brown paper cane; left hand empty. Bé Na: right hand empty; left hand empty.

Negative constraints: no smooth vector animation, no 3D rendering, no clay, no felt, no plastic, no glossy surfaces, no paper folding into 3D shapes, no bending or rubbery limbs, no characters turning in 3D, no drawn ink outlines, no photorealistic humans, no extra limbs, no duplicate characters, no knees or elbows bending backward, no limbs changing length, no head turned past the shoulders, no swapped left and right hands, no prop switching hands without a visible pass, no visible words, letters or numbers, no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes, no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no watermark.
```
