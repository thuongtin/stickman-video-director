### Clip 1 — The first card
Hands at start — Pip: right hand holds the red paper card; left hand empty.
[0–3s] Pip hops in from the left, the red paper card held up in his right hand.
[3–7s] He waves with his left hand while the card stays in his right hand.
[7–10s] He passes the card to his left hand and points ahead with his right hand.
Hands at end — Pip: right hand empty; left hand holds the red paper card.

### Clip 2 — Laying the path
Hands at start — Pip: right hand empty; left hand holds the red paper card.
[0–3s] Pip walks right, the red paper card swinging in his left hand.
[3–7s] He kneels and places the card on the path.
[7–10s] He stands and waves.
Hands at end — Pip: right hand empty; left hand empty.
