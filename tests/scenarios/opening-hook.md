# Opening hook behavioral cases

These are review cases, not records of completed model/provider runs. Score them against the Golden Hook section of `references/storyboard-template.md` and rubric items PLAN-6 and PACK-7.

1. Style 1 Dark, 60s, overthinking copy (the `examples.md` source): Phase A gives a complete hook card. The first frame shows the figure already under the thought tangle instead of standing beside a dot that appears; the first spoken line is a statement of about four to ten words, not "Do you ever…".
2. Style 1 Light, 30s money explainer asking for a "shocking number" opener: the hook uses Scale shock or Accent ignition with icon-only symbols; the number goes to the post-production overlay list at the hook timestamp, never into the prompt.
3. Style 2A, 9:16 product tip: Before → after cut with the glass interface elements; hook subject and hit sit in the central safe area.
4. Style 3, 30s cave satire: the cold open is the joke setup (Reaction before cause or deadpan Visual metaphor); no bass drop; the punchline payoff clip is named.
5. Style 4, 60s gentle anxiety story: Partial reveal or Satisfying motion with a soft sound cue; the first segment's location is recognizable in the first frame.
6. User asks for "a viral TikTok hook with a bass drop and flashing text": the skill keeps no-text and no-strobe rules, offers Silence then hit with one impact, and lists optional overlay text separately.
7. 3-minute documentary: the premise may build over 5–15 seconds, but the first two seconds still follow the hook card.
8. Phase B: Clip 1's first-frame state is the hook's first frame and its first beat times the hit at about 2s or earlier; no prompt has a separate `Opening hook:` line. `python3 tests/verify-opening-hook.py package.md` passes.
9. Optional ending loop for a 9:16 short: named in Phase A and the stitching guide; the final frame mirrors the first frame while the discussion question plays.
