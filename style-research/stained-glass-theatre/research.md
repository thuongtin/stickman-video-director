# Stained-glass theatre: research, not approved

Date: 22 October 2026. Base: 03b6f9ffdd399d9732f66875cf03abf8fca60a5e.

## Why a new direction
The nearest pack is paper-cutout. This direction uses hard dark lead seams and coloured transmitted light, not matte paper fibres or stacked contact shadows. Its material and lighting logic matter more than palette. It is not a revival of a voxel proposal. The demo is an original 2D vector-like glass puppet; it does not reproduce a museum work or claim physically simulated optical glass.

## Reference coverage
Three distinct works were researched via museum text/object records, read 22 October 2026. No reference artwork was downloaded or included. No video was watched. Object records are not licences to reproduce the objects.

1. V&A, Entry into Jerusalem, Germany about1375, C.200-1912. https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/how-was-it-made-stained-glass (updated17 April2024). Read process section: cartoon, cut coloured panes, painted details, lead joining. Adopt construction logic, not layout or iconography.
2. The Met, Autumn Landscape, attributed Agnes F. Northrop, Tiffany Studios,1923–24,25.173a–o. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/282. Read object and technique notes: glass texture, confetti and plating. Adopt material variation as an aspiration; the current code only implements tonal panes, fine seeds and edge glints, not this glass process.
3. Art Institute of Chicago, America Windows, Marc Chagall,1977. https://archive.artic.edu/chagall/3.4.html. Read six-panel record: deep blue tonal structure and art-related panels. Adopt broad dark-blue surround only. No figures, symbols or composition copied.

## Signature checklist
- MUST: dark lead boundaries divide hard glass facets.
- MUST: adult elongated articulated silhouette, not chibi sticker.
- MUST: transmitted coloured light separates this from matte paper.
- BONUS: restrained texture and glass irregularity. Current implementation uses stable fine seeds and tonal variation, not optical refraction.
- FORBIDDEN: copied window composition, religious symbol, logo, letters, photorealistic person.

Text policy: none. Camera: locked wide frame. Cadence: smooth eased poses sampled at12fps. Two arms/two legs, own-right gold wrist ring, no morph/hand swap. Limits: front view, planted stance, empty hands. No walk, prop transfer, side view or multi-character collision is certified by this demo.
